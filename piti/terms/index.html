2. The service

Piti is a field journal for butterfly and moth surveys. It lets you:

  • Plan seven-day survey weeks and follow them from planned to surveying, logged, ready, completed and archived
  • Log sightings by life stage (egg, larva, pupa, adult) with species, host plant, notes, weather text, plot quadrant, photos, video clips and voice memos
  • Review pending identifications, browse a host plant herbarium, and view life-stage census summaries
  • Compose survey reels in the Studio from your media using reel templates, ambient background scores and voiceovers, and export them to your Photos library
  • Keep a private Field Log of photo, clip and voice-note records with place, species, host plant and life stage, and star the ones you want at the top

Species names, host plant notes and census summaries in Piti are based on what you enter. Piti does not verify identifications and is not a scientific authority.
