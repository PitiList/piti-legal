Piti Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Piti iOS app (bundle ID com.piti.zgsuo, "Piti", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the app. Piti is a field journal for butterfly and moth surveys: you plan seven-day survey weeks, log sightings of eggs, larvae, pupae and adults, note host plants, record voice memos, turn a week into a short survey reel, and keep a private Field Log of your observations.

If you have any question about this policy, contact us at PitiList@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

When you tap Enter Piti on the Welcome screen, Piti creates an account for you automatically (Quick Login). No email address, phone number, real name, or password entry is required. The account consists of:

  • A randomly generated user ID, username and password created by the app
  • A display name, title and affiliation (pre-filled, editable in Profile)
  • An optional profile photo you choose from your photo library

1.2 Content you create

  • Survey weeks: title, site name, date range, target count, notes and status
  • Sightings: life stage, species name, host plant, notes, weather text and plot quadrant you type in, and the time you saved the sighting
  • Photos and video clips you capture with the in-app camera or import from your photo library
  • Voice memos and voiceovers you record, and text produced when you dictate notes
  • Survey reels you export in the Studio
  • Field Log records you create or edit, including observation notes, place names, species, host plants, life stages and attached photos, clips and voice notes

1.3 Interaction and preference data

  • Field Log records you starred
  • Appearance setting (System, Light or Dark) and stage-label preference
  • Your Wing Reel Credits balance, the one-time welcome credit flag, and App Store transaction identifiers used to avoid adding the same purchase twice
  • Whether the notification permission prompt has already been shown

1.4 Information we do not collect

Piti does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, health data, browsing history, advertising identifier, or payment card details. Piti does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the features you ask for:

  • Show and organize your survey weeks, sightings, host plants and life-stage counts
  • Display your photos, clips and voice memos and play them back
  • Compose and export survey reels that you request, and save them to your Photos library when you allow it
  • Show your Field Log records in the order you choose (starred first, then newest)
  • Maintain your Wing Reel Credits balance and deliver purchased credits
  • Respond to support requests you send us by email

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where information is kept

Your account, survey records, Field Log records, media and settings are kept in Piti's private app storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS app sandboxing. They are not copied to other users' devices. Exported reels are additionally saved to your Photos library when you grant access, and are then managed by the Photos app.

4. Facial data

This chapter is Piti's explanation of how facial data is collected, used, disclosed, shared, and retained, as referenced for App Store Review Guideline 2.1 and 5.1.1.

4.1 What is collected

Piti does not collect, derive, or store biometric facial identifiers, faceprints, face geometry, or face-recognition templates, and does not use Face ID data. Piti does not perform face detection, face recognition, or facial analysis of any kind.

Photos and video clips that you choose to capture or import (for example, a profile photo or a field photo that happens to include a person) may depict a human face. Piti treats these only as ordinary images you selected.

4.2 How it is used

Such images are used only to show them back to you inside Piti (profile avatar, sighting attachments, media library, Field Log records) and to include them in survey reels that you explicitly export. They are not used for identification, authentication, profiling, advertising, or training any model.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

Piti does not send these images to us or to any third party. They are shared only when you choose to: by saving an exported reel to your Photos library, or by using the iOS share sheet from the Export Complete screen. Images are stored in Piti's private app storage on your iPhone, as described in Section 3.

4.4 Retention

An image is kept until you delete it (delete the sighting, attachment, field record or reel, or remove your profile photo), use Clear All Data, use Delete Account, or uninstall Piti. Copies you saved to your Photos library or shared elsewhere are controlled by you outside Piti.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

Sections 4.1 to 4.4 are the complete explanation of how Piti collects, uses, discloses, shares, and retains images that may contain faces.

6. Third-party services and automated processing

Piti does not use any third-party automated processing or model service. No prompts, photos, videos, audio, or text are sent to an external model provider. Reel composition and ambient background scores run inside the app.

Apple provides the App Store payment system (Section 7), push notification permission, and the Speech framework (Section 5). Their handling of data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • Edit: change your display name, title, affiliation and profile photo in Profile.
  • Delete individual items: delete sightings, attachments, voice memos, survey weeks, stored reels and Field Log records from their screens.
  • Clear Cache: removes thumbnails and temporary files.
  • Clear All Data: permanently deletes all survey weeks, sightings, Field Log records, photos, clips, voice memos and stored reels. Your profile and Wing Reel Credits are kept.
  • Log Out: ends your session and returns to the Welcome screen. Your data is kept and is available when you tap Enter Piti again.
  • Delete Account (Profile > Settings): after confirmation, permanently deletes your account credentials and profile, profile photo, all survey data and media, stored reels, your Field Log records and stars, your settings, and your Wing Reel Credits balance and welcome-credit flag. Unused credits cannot be recovered after deletion. Copies you saved to your Photos library or emails you sent are not affected.

Because your data is kept in Piti's app storage on your iPhone, we cannot retrieve or restore it for you after deletion.

9. Data security

Piti relies on iOS app sandboxing and device protections (such as your passcode) to protect stored data. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device protected.

10. Children's privacy

Piti is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used Piti, contact us and we will help remove their information.

11. International users

Piti is available in multiple regions. Information is handled as described in this policy regardless of where you use the app.
